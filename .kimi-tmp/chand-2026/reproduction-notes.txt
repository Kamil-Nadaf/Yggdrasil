# Reproducing the Fermi-LAT results of Chand et al. (arXiv:2608.06527) with our pipeline

Status as of 2026-11-04. Short summary of what was done, what reproduces, and what is still open.

## 1. Goal

Check that our generic LAT upper-limit pipeline (`instruments/lat_ul_worker.py`, Fermitools unbinned likelihood) reproduces the paper's LAT results:
a positive control (GRB 200415A, detected) and six negative candidates (GRB 081213A, 180128A, 231024A, 120616A, 200423A, 231115A).

## 2. Setup used (identical for every event, nothing tuned per event)

- Fermitools 2.5.3 (paper: 2.2.0), fermipy 1.4.3, native ARM container.
- Event class P8R3_TRANSIENT020E_V3 for the 10^2 s window, P8R3_TRANSIENT010E_V3 for 10^3 and 10^4 s; FRONT+BACK; zmax 100 deg.
- 12 deg ROI around the host galaxy, 0.1-100 GeV fit range, gll_iem_v07, matching isotropic template, gll_psc_v35 catalogue (paper does not state the 4FGL-DR4 version).
- Power law with index fixed to -2 for limits; fluxes quoted in 0.1-10 GeV. Sources within 5 deg free; NGC 253 (a 4FGL source at the target) frozen at its catalogue flux (decision D1).
- Windows: T0 to T0+100, 1000, 10000 s after the GBM trigger. Trigger times from HEASARC, converted to MET with leap seconds.

## 3. Results

### 3.1 Exposure (fixed a real preprocessing error)

`gtmktime` had been run with `roicut=no`. Requiring the whole 12 deg ROI to lie inside the 100 deg zenith cut (target zenith < 88 deg, i.e. `roicut=yes`) reproduces the paper's exposure notes:

| Quantity | Ours | Paper |
|---|---|---|
| first exposure, 180128A / 120616A / 200423A | 147 / 1445 / 618 s | 148 / 1447 / 619 s |
| GTI 081213A (10^3 / 10^4 s) | 281 / 2840 s | 281 / 2840 s |
| GTI 180128A, 200415A, 120616A, 231024A | 5520, 4170, 4200, 1000 s | 5520, 4170, 4200, 1000 s |
| "exposure = 0" cases (180128A 1e2; 120616A 1e2, 1e3; 200423A 1e2) | NO_EXPOSURE | exposure = 0 |

Only GRB 231115A at 10^4 s differs (ours 3984 s GTI, paper "exposure ~6.1 ks"; possibly a different quantity).

### 3.3 Derived quantity (Table 4, LAT column)

## 4. What we learned

1. **The pipeline works.** With correct exposure it reproduces the paper's exposure notes, the negative result, the energy-flux limits to about 15%, and the positive control's flux and spectral index.
2. **Upper-limit definition matters.** The paper says its limits are profile likelihood with delta TS = 2.71. Our profile limits at 2dlnL = 2.71 are about 2.5x tighter, while a Bayesian 95% limit (flat prior in flux) matches. A profile limit at 2dlnL = 5.42 (reading "delta TS = 2.71" as delta lnL = 2.71) is being tested next. Until that is settled, both definitions are kept and any quoted limit must state which was used.
3. **Generic worker fixes** (all recorded in `docs/OPEN_ISSUES.md`): keep the FT1 file's own acceptance cone; retry chain for Minuit non-convergence; "unconstrained" status for zero-exposure windows; leap-second-aware MET conversion.
4. **Paper inconsistencies noticed:** the stacked limit uses 4 or 5 events depending on the section; the paper's own flux error for 200415A implies about 1.8 sigma while its TS is 25.
